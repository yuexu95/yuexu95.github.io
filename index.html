Dr. Yue Xu is an Instructor at Baylor College of Medicine at Yong Li’s lab since October 2025 and completed his UHN-PRiME postdoctoral fellowship in Bowen Li’s lab at the Leslie Dan Faculty of Pharmacy, University of Toronto. He was trained in medicinal chemistry, earning a Ph.D. from China Pharmaceutical University, and during his postdoctoral training, he brought a computational perspective to experimental science, developing predictive models and analyses to guide synthesis and biological testing. His current work focuses on building a high-throughput screening platform using rational design to evaluate lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) for mRNA delivery.
